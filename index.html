            Backup

            Your collection is stored on this device. Download a backup regularly; your own cover photos are included. “Restore a file” also accepts a collection import file, which is merged with your existing data.

            Export

            Printable list with cover, title, artist, year, performers, label and total duration (no track lists). In the print dialog, choose “Save as PDF”.

            Discogs

            Free: discogs.com → Settings → Developers → “Generate new token”.

            AI photo reading

            Stored on this device only. Free option: Gemini (aistudio.google.com). Without a key, reading goes through the Claude app.

            Advanced: provider and model

            Set automatically by provider. Change only if the model was renamed or you prefer another (it must support image input).
